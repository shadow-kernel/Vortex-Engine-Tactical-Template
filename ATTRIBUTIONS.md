**Modifications** made for this template (the licence asks to indicate changes): the packs' scene helpers
(background plane, aim markers, sky dome) were removed; the glTF skin data was repaired (inverse bind matrices
converted to the glTF metre space; the UZI's per-axis vertex distortion from the FBX→glTF conversion undone); the
content was mirrored on X for the engine's left-handed space; degenerate triangles were removed; textures were
re-encoded at 2K; every animation was extracted to `.vanim` clips, resampled to 60 fps and keyframe-reduced.
